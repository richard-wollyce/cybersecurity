# Cybersecurity Essentials

Welcome to **Cybersecurity Essentials**.

This path was created for people who want a clear, practical introduction to cybersecurity before moving into deeper technical and strategic topics. Here, the focus is on building strong foundations, understanding the language of the field, and developing the mindset needed to navigate digital risks with confidence.

## What this path covers

Throughout this essentials path, the content will briefly introduce topics such as:

- What cybersecurity is and why it matters.
- The main types of digital threats, such as phishing, malware, scams, credential theft, and social engineering.
- Core security concepts like confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, and access control.
- Basic personal and professional protection practices, including strong passwords, password managers, multi-factor authentication, safe browsing, software updates, backups, and device hygiene.
- How attackers usually think, operate, and exploit human and technical weaknesses.
- Introductory ideas around networks, systems, data exposure, and incident response.
- The difference between learning cybersecurity for awareness, for professional growth, and for more advanced technical practice.

## How the website works

This website is organized as a learning path.

- The **Essentials** path is the starting point and focuses on accessible, beginner-friendly content.
- The **Advanced** path will go deeper into technical subjects, analysis, architecture, defense strategies, and more specialized security topics.
- Each page is designed to be short, structured, and easy to revisit.
- The goal is to help you progress step by step, so you can build knowledge without feeling overwhelmed.

## What to expect

The purpose of this path is not to overload you with jargon, but to give you a practical map of cybersecurity as a field. As you move through the lessons, you will understand the main ideas, common risks, and essential habits that you should develop either as a user or a developer.
