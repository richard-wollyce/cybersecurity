## Presenter

Richard Wollyce is a Software Engineer and Secure Systems Architect, and the creator of Road To Cybersec.  

## What participants learn

- Core cybersecurity and digital risk fundamentals, using the Road To Cybersec content.  
- Cyber hygiene and account protection: passwords, MFA, phishing, and safer browsing habits.  
- Secure use of devices and cloud services, including laptops, smartphones, and SaaS tools in a work context.  
- Introduction to secure web development and common vulnerabilities, explained in clear language for both technical and non-technical teams.

## Format

- On-site, instructor-led workshop with guided discussions and real-world examples.  
- Content tailored to the audience level, such as engineering teams, leadership, or general staff.  
- Duration and depth adjusted to your specific goals and time constraints.
